A practical checklist for maintaining a clean and organized commercial office environment.

Daily Office Cleaning

Empty waste bins

Dust accessible surfaces

Vacuum carpeted areas

Sweep or mop hard floors

Clean frequently touched surfaces

Clean reception areas

Check meeting rooms

Clean kitchen and break-room surfaces

Clean restrooms

Check entrances and common areas

Weekly Office Cleaning

Perform detailed dusting

Clean corners and edges

Clean doors and frames

Clean internal glass

Detail meeting-room surfaces

Perform detailed kitchen cleaning

Inspect carpets and flooring

Clean less frequently accessed areas

Periodic Office Maintenance

Depending on the workplace, periodic maintenance may include:

Carpet cleaning
Floor maintenance
Detailed window cleaning
High-level dusting
Furniture cleaning
Difficult-to-reach areas
Office Cleaning Tips

Keep work areas organized and remove food waste promptly. Spills should be addressed as soon as practical, while cleaning products and methods should be appropriate for the surfaces being maintained.

Cleaning frequency should be adjusted according to office size, occupancy, traffic levels, and business requirements.
