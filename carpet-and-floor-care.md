Carpet and Floor Care

Commercial floors and carpets experience regular foot traffic and require appropriate maintenance.

Carpet Care

Regular carpet maintenance may include:

Routine vacuuming
Prompt attention to spills
Entrance mat maintenance
Spot cleaning
Periodic deeper cleaning

Regular maintenance can help keep carpeted areas looking presentable and can reduce the buildup of loose dirt and debris.

Hard Floor Care

Hard flooring can include tile, vinyl, concrete, timber, laminate, and other materials.

Routine maintenance may include:

Removing loose dirt
Sweeping or vacuuming
Mopping
Spot cleaning
Periodic maintenance

Cleaning products and methods should be appropriate for the particular flooring material.

Entrance Areas

Entrances often receive significant foot traffic. Regular attention to entrance floors and mats can help reduce the amount of dirt carried into other areas of a building.

Spill Management

Spills should be addressed promptly using an appropriate method for the surface. Businesses should follow relevant product and flooring manufacturer recommendations.

Professional Maintenance

Some carpets and floors may require specialist cleaning or periodic maintenance depending on their material, condition, and level of use.
