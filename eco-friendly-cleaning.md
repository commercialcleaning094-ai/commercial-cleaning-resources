Eco-Friendly Commercial Cleaning Practices

Businesses can consider environmentally responsible approaches as part of their workplace cleaning programs.

Reducing Unnecessary Chemical Use

Use cleaning products only when appropriate and follow the manufacturer's instructions for dilution and application.

Reducing Water Waste

Where suitable, cleaning procedures can be planned to avoid unnecessary water consumption.

Reusable Materials

Reusable cleaning materials may be considered where appropriate, provided they can be properly cleaned and maintained.

Waste Management

Businesses can support responsible waste management by:

Using appropriate waste containers
Avoiding overflowing bins
Separating waste where suitable
Keeping waste areas clean
Following workplace waste procedures
Choosing Cleaning Products

Businesses may consider environmentally preferable products where they meet the cleaning requirements of the workplace and are suitable for the surfaces being maintained.

Efficient Equipment

Appropriately selected and maintained cleaning equipment can support efficient workplace cleaning.

Practical Approach

Environmental considerations should always be balanced with effective cleaning, workplace requirements, safety considerations, surface compatibility, and manufacturer recommendations.

A sustainable cleaning program should be practical and appropriate for the individual workplace.
