Commercial Cleaning Schedule

A structured cleaning schedule helps businesses organize routine workplace maintenance.

Daily Cleaning

Typical daily tasks may include:

Emptying waste bins
Cleaning high-touch surfaces
Cleaning restrooms
Cleaning kitchen surfaces
Vacuuming high-traffic areas
Sweeping and mopping hard floors
Checking entrances
Cleaning common areas
Several Times Per Week

Depending on workplace usage:

Detailed vacuuming
Kitchen maintenance
Common-area cleaning
Additional restroom cleaning
Spot cleaning
High-traffic floor maintenance
Weekly Cleaning

Weekly activities may include:

Detailed dusting
Cleaning corners and edges
Cleaning doors and frames
Cleaning internal glass
Detailed kitchen cleaning
Detailed floor cleaning
Reviewing less frequently accessed areas
Monthly or Periodic Cleaning

Some activities can be scheduled periodically:

Carpet cleaning
Floor maintenance
Detailed window cleaning
High-level dusting
Difficult-to-reach areas
Detailed furniture cleaning
Adjusting the Schedule

There is no single cleaning schedule suitable for every business. Frequency should be considered according to workplace size, occupancy, business type, traffic levels, operating hours, flooring, and specific cleaning requirements.
